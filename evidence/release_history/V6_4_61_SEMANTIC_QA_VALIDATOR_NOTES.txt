DocuBot v6.4.61 — Semantic QA Validator Correction
==================================================

Scope
-----
This release changes the Option-C technical QA harness only. Production answer,
retrieval, indexing, chunking, model, grounding, and UI behavior are unchanged.

Reason
------
The v6.4.60 office run produced a grounded Rule 16.6 answer that stated the
requirement was satisfied and that two switch-clauses meet the minimum of two,
but OC-015 still required the literal token "Yes.". That created a false
negative even though the answer semantics were correct.

Correction
----------
OC-015 now requires:
- Rule 16.6 evidence;
- one satisfaction semantic (for example: Yes, satisfies, satisfied, meets the
  requirement, or no violation established); and
- one minimum-two semantic (minimum of 2, minimum of two, or at least two).

The source and no-unnecessary-LLM requirements remain unchanged.

No production behavior change
-----------------------------
No changes are made to AnswerService, retriever, reranker, embeddings, Qdrant,
BM25, ingestion, chunking, prompts, grounding, or UI.
