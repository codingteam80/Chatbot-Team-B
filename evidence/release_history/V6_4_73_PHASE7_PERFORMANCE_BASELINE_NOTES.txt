DocuBot v6.4.73 — Phase 7 Performance Baseline
================================================

Scope
-----
Profiling/tooling only. The certified v6.4.72 production-v4 runtime remains the
quality baseline. This release does not change the LLM, embedding model,
reranker, retrieval thresholds, Top-K, chunking profile, index schema, prompts,
answer logic, documents, or production Qdrant data.

Why
---
The v6.4.70/v6.4.72 certified evidence shows three different ~30 second paths:
- OC-007: ~32.2 s total with ~32.18 s inside one Qwen2.5 generation call.
- EN-007: ~32.5 s total with ~3.05 s retrieval and ~29.45 s across two LLM calls.
- OC-011: ~31.85 s total entirely in retrieval before strict fallback.

Therefore Phase 7 starts with stage-level measurement rather than generic
"speed tweaks".

One-click office benchmark
--------------------------
Run:
    Run_Phase7_Performance_Baseline.bat

It requires the promoted v4 production baseline and a passing Production
Architecture Lock. It measures exact fast control, first/warm OOD retrieval,
first/warm OC-007, and first/warm EN-007 in one shared AnswerService process.
It records existing request/LLM/lazy-load events plus non-invasive timings for
BM25, Ollama embedding API, vector search, and hybrid merge. Reranker cold-load
is taken from the existing certified lazy-component event; first-vs-repeat
retrieval residual shows steady-state rerank/candidate cost without importing
Torch early and contaminating the cold measurement.
