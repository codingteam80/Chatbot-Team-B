Purpose
-------
Remove the remaining measured 25-second-class OC-008 Qwen long tail on lower-spec PCs without broadening deterministic review routing indiscriminately.

Change
------
A single-rule MISRA reviewer answer may skip Qwen only when ALL of these are true:
- the current turn contains visible C/C++ code;
- exactly one citable structured MISRA Rule/Directive candidate is deterministically matched;
- the user explicitly constrains the review not to invent/assume facts, or to use only visible/shown/provided evidence;
- deterministic cue coverage is at least 0.95;
- the deterministic state is definite (compliant or violation), never uncertain;
- a visible-code observation exists;
- the deterministic answer passes the existing reference-grounding and semantic-alignment guards.

Ordinary single-rule reviewer questions continue to use the existing Qwen route for naturalness. Multi-rule Optimization #1 routing is unchanged.
