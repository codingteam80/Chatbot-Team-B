DocuBot v6.4.74 — Phase 7 Optimization #1 Trial
================================================

Purpose
-------
Reduce the two largest measured wastes from the v6.4.73 lower-spec profiling
baseline without changing models, retrieval thresholds, Top-K, Chunking-v4,
Qdrant data, or the source-grounding contract.

Optimization A — self-verified deterministic MISRA reviewer finalization
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
For visible-code multi-rule MISRA reviews, DocuBot first builds the existing deterministic
assessment. The LLM is skipped only if that answer:
  1) is built from strong structured/rule-body evidence,
  2) cites only accepted references, and
  3) passes the existing generated_assessment_is_aligned semantic guard.

This directly targets EN-007 and OC-007, where profiling showed 10–30 seconds
spent on Qwen generation even though deterministic visible-code states were
already complete.

Optimization B — conservative identity-only OOD fast fail
---------------------------------------------------------
For identity-style questions such as "Who was Jose Rizal?", after the existing
BM25 identity fast path fails, DocuBot may skip vector/reranker loading only when:
  - the requested subject contains at least two meaningful tokens, and
  - none of those tokens appears anywhere in the active company corpus or source
    titles.

Any token footprint, non-identity wording, compound/list intent, or source-scoped
retrieval keeps the normal certified semantic path.

Trial
-----
Run_Phase7_Optimization1_Trial.bat

  logs\phase7_optimization1\DocuBot_Phase7_Optimization1_Result_*.zip

The trial reuses the same seven v6.4.73 scenarios and requires both the existing
quality guards and the new optimization-activation guards to pass.
