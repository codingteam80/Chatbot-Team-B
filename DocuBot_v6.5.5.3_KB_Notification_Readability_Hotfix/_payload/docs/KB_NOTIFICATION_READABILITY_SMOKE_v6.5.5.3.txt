DocuBot v6.5.5.3 — KB Notification Readability Smoke
=====================================================

Purpose
-------
Visual-only hotfix for the successful Update Knowledge Base toast.

Expected success toast
----------------------
Knowledge Base is successfully updated.
Added: <n> · Updated: <n> · Deleted: <n>

Acceptance
----------
- Entire title is readable.
- Added / Updated / Deleted counts are readable; no bottom clipping.
- Toast remains top-right and does not move the welcome/chat layout.
- Chat remains disabled while the KB update is in progress.
- Incremental KB behavior is unchanged.
- Technical details remain in KB update logs only.
