DocuBot v6.4.4 - Context, Multilingual Retrieval, and Grounding Recovery
=======================================================================

Purpose
-------
Validate the focused v6.4.4 recovery without making the chatbot dependent on
history documents or any one domain. Use the fixed regressions plus fresh
questions drawn from whatever supported documents are actually in the folder.

A. Fixed regression - person/entity continuity
-----------------------------------------------
1. Who is Emilio Aguinaldo?
2. What role did he hold in the First Philippine Republic?
3. Why was he important?

Expected behavior:
- Turn 3 must still resolve "he" to Emilio Aguinaldo, even if retrieval after
  turn 2 changed the broad current topic.
- No answer should come from outside the indexed folder/storage.

Fresh chain variant:
1. Who is Apolinario Mabini?
2. What position did he hold?
3. Why was he significant?

B. Fixed regression - Tagalog relation retrieval against English content
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Kailan nilagdaan ang Treaty of Paris ng 1898?
2. When was the Treaty of Paris of 1898 signed?

Expected behavior:
- Both should retrieve the same supported signing-date fact when that source is
  present in the indexed storage.
- Evidence should show a deterministic multilingual relation normalization for
  the Tagalog question when applicable.
- Do not lower the global 0.55 retrieval threshold.

Fresh cross-domain variants to run only when matching source facts exist:
- Ilang araw ang sick leave na ibinibigay?
- Sino ang nag-aapruba ng leave request?
- Kailan nagsimula ang process/policy/event named in another stored document?

C. Compound facet cleanup
-------------------------
Question:
How much sick leave is provided and who must approve a leave request?

Expected evidence:
- Compound facet 1: how much sick leave is provided
- Compound facet 2: who must approve a leave request
- The second facet must not contain residue such as "much sick leave provided".

D. Verifier grounding / latency regression
-------------------------------------------
1. What is Section 6? Explain it as well.
2. What is the Katipunan and explain why it was important.

Expected behavior:
- If the first grounded draft already covers every explicit facet, the compound
  verifier is skipped.
- A verifier must never expand a conservative draft with unsupported claims.
- In the Section 6 corpus used in prior testing, do not introduce statements
  such as "Advisory guidelines are less critical" because the source explicitly
  says the categories do not imply a grading of importance.
- Compare total latency with v6.4.3 evidence; an already-covered compound answer
  should avoid the unnecessary second Qwen call.

E. Domain-neutral fresh testing rule
------------------------------------
For every update, add NEW questions and NEW conversation chains from different
files currently present in the configured folder/storage. Do not make all tests
about MISRA, Philippine history, or leave policy. Suggested capability coverage:
- exact fact/date/quantity
- definition/overview
- person/entity follow-up
- object/section/rule follow-up
- Tagalog-to-English-source retrieval
- same-turn compound intent
- cross-document retrieval
- missing-information fallback
- citation/source traceability

Pass conditions
---------------
- No hardcoded document/topic answer logic.
- No external knowledge.
- Unsupported questions keep the exact fallback behavior.
- Existing exact structured fast path remains intact.
- No global threshold/model/embedding change.
- No warnings-as-errors, syntax, compile, packaging, or regression failures.
