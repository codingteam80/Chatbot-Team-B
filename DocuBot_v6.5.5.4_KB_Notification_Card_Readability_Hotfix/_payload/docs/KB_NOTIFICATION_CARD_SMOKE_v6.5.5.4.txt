DocuBot v6.5.5.4 KB Notification Card Smoke Test
=================================================

Purpose
-------
Fix the browser/Streamlit-dependent clipping of the successful KB update toast
without changing the already-working incremental KB update or chat-disable flow.

Expected success notification
-----------------------------
Knowledge Base is successfully updated.
Added: X   Updated: Y   Deleted: Z

The success notification is a DocuBot-owned fixed top-right card. It must:
- show the full title;
- show all three counts clearly;
- not expose chunks, Qdrant/BM25, semantic-index or KB Health detail;
- not move the welcome/chat layout;
- disappear automatically after about 12 seconds;
- leave failure notifications on the existing warning-toast path.

During KB update
----------------
- Chat textarea remains disabled.
- Send action remains disabled/server-guarded.
- Maintenance panel and chat/welcome positions remain stable.

Architecture preserved
----------------------
- Unchanged files: SKIP.
- New/modified/deleted: process incrementally.
- Qwen2.5 generation, Qwen3 embeddings, BGE reranker, Qdrant + BM25 preserved.
- Chunking 900/150, score threshold 0.55, Top-K 10/10/3 preserved.
- No production KB rebuild is performed by this installer.
